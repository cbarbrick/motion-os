Motion OS privacy notice

Updated November 3, 2026

Motion OS is Motion’s shared workspace for tasks, weekly deliverables, events, content plans, athletes, affiliate proposals, potential collaborators, ideas and personal to-dos.

Account and workspace information

We use your account identifier, email address and display name to sign you in and associate work with you. Google sign-in requests basic identity information (name, email and profile). Motion OS does not request access to your Gmail messages or Google Drive files. A workspace owner may separately connect Google Calendar to create a dedicated Motion OS calendar. This optional connection requests access only to calendars created by this app, not the owner’s unrelated calendars.

The workspace stores the records you enter, task assignments, completion states and an activity history of shared-work updates. Other workspace members can view and edit shared records. Athlete, affiliate and people profiles include submitted contact details, descriptions, rates, proposals, comments and dated review decisions. The active athlete roster has no review stage. David’s approval stores athlete, project, person, affiliate and other requests, including descriptions, budgets, supporting links, comments and decision history. Only the selected reviewer approves or denies these requests. Athlete events and their content requirements are shared with workspace members. Notifications store who added an event, deliverable or available task, or assigned you to a task, along with the item, time and whether you read the alert. Notifications are visible only to their recipient through the app. Personal to-dos and their photos are visible through the app only to their creator. Photos attached to shared tasks and deliverables are available to current workspace members. Uploaded photos are resized and converted to JPEG without the original metadata, and stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket. Removing a photo hides it and allows undo; the stored file is retained until it is permanently removed by an administrator. The workspace owner manages membership and invite links.

Shared Google Calendar

After the owner connects Google, event titles, descriptions, locations and dates entered by workspace members are copied to the dedicated Motion OS Google calendar. Updates in Motion OS update the same Google event. All current workspace members can view and edit these events through Motion OS. Event guests are workspace labels; the sync does not send Google guest invitations. Changes made directly in Google are not imported into Motion OS.

The Google refresh credential is encrypted and stored privately on the server to keep syncing when members are offline. It is never sent to other workspace members. The owner can revoke the app’s Google connection in their Google account permissions; this stops syncing but leaves previously saved events in both services. Sample events never sync.

Invitations

Anyone who receives an active workspace invite link can sign in and join that workspace. Share the link only with intended teammates. The owner can replace or turn off the link and remove members.

Services and storage

Netlify hosts the app. Supabase provides authentication and stores workspace data. Google authenticates users who choose Google sign-in. When email sign-in is enabled, the configured email delivery service sends verification and account recovery messages. These providers process information needed to operate the service.

Your browser or installed home-screen app stores its own sign-in session, unsaved editor drafts and a pending invitation. Home-screen Google sign-in uses a temporary return code through Netlify. The return request expires after ten minutes and is removed after sign-in. Abandoned return codes are purged hourly. The return service does not store your access token, refresh token or the secret needed to exchange the code. Sample-mode records and sample photos are stored in that browser. Unsaved photo selections are kept only while the editor is open; text drafts do not include those files. The offline page does not provide offline access to shared workspace records. Signing out ends the local session; saved drafts may remain on that device. Use a private browser session on shared devices.

Retention and your requests

Workspace records remain saved until they are removed or the workspace is retired. Removing a member does not automatically erase their contributions or activity history. You can export the records available to your account from Team & access.

For account access, corrections or deletion requests, contact char@motionbrand.com. The workspace owner can arrange the requested changes, including records not removable in the app.
